## Combining About + Contact

Your instinct here is *correct*.

For small, intentional studios, **About + Contact being separate often creates friction**, not clarity.

### Why combining them works for *you* specifically:

* Your “About” is relational, not corporate
* Your “Contact” is personal, not transactional
* Visitors who want to reach out almost always want context *first*

### Structure that works beautifully:

**Page title:**

> *About Fairweather*

**Sections:**

1. **Who we are** (short, warm, human)
2. **How we work** (process, care, printing, intention)
3. **Why this matters to us** (one paragraph, not a manifesto)
4. **Reach out** (contact form, email, gentle invitation)

This removes a decision point for the visitor *and* for you.

Fewer doors. Same room.
