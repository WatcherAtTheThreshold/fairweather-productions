# Fairweather Print Business: Financial Overview
*Handcrafted Art Prints - Business Model & Financial Analysis*

---

## Executive Summary

**Business Model:** Handcrafted art print production and sales using desktop printing technology that achieves professional Giclee-quality results at accessible price points.

**Current Status:** 
- Operating at Maia Papaya coffee shop (Littleton, NH)
- 10 paintings on display, December through January
- Prints selling at $25 each (91% margin)
- Preparing for online sales launch at $50 per shipped print (61% margin)

**Investment Need:** Minimal - business is bootstrapped and profitable from day one. Any investment would accelerate materials inventory and online launch.

---

## Business Model Overview

### Production Method
- **Printing:** Desktop inkjet printer with Staples Ultra Premium Matte Photo Paper
- **Quality:** Matches expensive Giclee proofs through developed technique
- **Format:** 8" x 10" prints matted to standard 11" x 14" frame size
- **Fulfillment:** Handcrafted printing, matting, and packaging per order
- **Innovation:** Months of R&D proving desktop printing can achieve professional quality

### Revenue Streams

**Current (December 2025):**
- Coffee shop consignment sales: $25 per print

**Launching Soon (Q1 2026):**
- Online direct sales: $50 per print (includes shipping)

**Future Potential:**
- Additional retail locations (coffee shops, boutiques, wellness centers)
- Complementary products (greeting cards, bookmarks, small prints)
- Seasonal limited editions
- Workshops on printing technique

## Financial Risks & Mitigation

### Risk: Printer Failure
**Impact:** Production stops, ~$300-400 replacement cost
**Mitigation:** 
- Regular maintenance schedule
- Emergency fund for replacement ($200-300 reserved)
- Backup plan: Staples print services for urgent orders
- Equipment replacement budgeted annually

### Risk: Slow Sales Periods
**Impact:** Lower revenue during off-season months
**Mitigation:**
- Low overhead means break-even at 1-2 prints per month
- Multiple revenue streams (coffee shop + online + additional locations)
- Seasonal planning and content calendar
- Email list for direct customer engagement

### Risk: Materials Cost Increases
**Impact:** Reduced margins if paper or mat costs rise
**Mitigation:**
- 60%+ margins provide significant buffer
- Alternative suppliers researched and documented
- Bulk purchasing during sales
- Can adjust retail pricing if necessary (market supports $60-75)

### Risk: Competition
**Impact:** Other artists in same venues or market
**Mitigation:**
- Unique handcrafted story and goddess themes
- Quality and presentation differentiate from mass-produced alternatives
- Customer relationships over transactional sales
- Continuous artistic evolution
